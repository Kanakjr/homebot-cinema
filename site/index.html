Features

Everything a household needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Stremio's player and addon catalogs, plus the pieces a shared home server needs.

Profiles

Everyone gets their own.

Up to eight profiles behind a "Who's watching?" screen, with avatars, an optional PIN and a kids mode. Each one keeps its own library, Continue watching, history, settings and accent colour.

  • Kids profiles get child-friendly results
  • Hide and reorder Home rows, per profile
  • Admin is a sign-in, not a profile

Discover

Find something to watch, fast.

Smart filters search by rating, votes, genre, language, runtime, year and "streaming on a service in your country". Forty quick picks cover charts, genres and short-on-time nights.

  • Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, IMDb and Letterboxd ratings
  • "More like this" and movie collections on title pages
  • Search puts the best match first, across every catalog

Watch

Press play. Keep watching.

One click resumes where you stopped or starts the next episode. Continue watching shows the time left or what's up next, and the Upcoming row lists episodes airing in the next two weeks.

  • Skip intro, recap and credits, with marks on the seek bar
  • One panel for audio and subtitles, with subtitle auto-sync
  • A deep 90 to 240 second buffer for smooth playback

Your server

New episodes arrive by themselves.

Save movies and shows to your own disk through Transmission. Sentinel follows the shows you pick, fetches each new episode in the quality you chose, and never re-downloads one you deleted.

  • Quality profiles: 4K, HD Balanced, Space Saver or your own
  • A swarm check tells you a release is alive before you start
  • The server library and Calendar show what is on disk

AI copilot · optional

Ask for ideas. You press the button.

Ask for "something light under two hours" or "is season 3 on the server?". The copilot answers with poster cards and can stage a download, but nothing starts until you confirm.

  • Sees titles only: never names, emails or PINs
  • Staged actions expire after ten minutes
  • Off by default; kids profiles can't stage anything

For the admin

Know what's playing.

See who is watching what, on which device, and whether it plays directly or is transcoded. Watch stats keep every play on your own server: hours per day, streaks, top titles, devices and a CSV export.

  • Switch plugins on and off in Admin › Plugins
  • People, quality profiles and an activity log
  • A daily database snapshot, the last seven kept

How it works

Runs on a box you own.

A Mac mini, a NAS or a mini PC. Cloudflare Access decides who can sign in, and everything else stays in one folder.

  • Fails closedNo valid Cloudflare Access token, no access. Only the people you invite can sign in.
  • Your data stays homeProfiles, history and settings live in one folder on your machine, never in someone else's cloud.
  • No sources shippedThe repository ships metadata, subtitle and public-domain addons only. You choose the rest.
  • Signed linksLinks for VLC and other players are signed and expire. Stream responses are never cached.

Get started

Up and running in five commands.

You need Docker with Compose, a folder for movies and one for shows.

Terminal
git clone https://github.com/Kanakjr/homebot-cinema.git && cd homebot-cinema
cp .env.example .env                              # fill in the values
cp data/addons.example.json data/addons.json      # addons every profile gets
cp docker-compose.example.yml docker-compose.yml  # point it at your folders
docker compose up -d --build

Then open http://localhost:3002, create the first profile, and you're in. Every setting is in the README.

Sign-in

On a real server, put it behind Cloudflare Access and set CF_TEAM_DOMAIN, CF_ACCESS_AUD and ADMIN_EMAILS.

Just trying it?

Uncomment the two local-trial lines in docker-compose.yml. Never on a server other people can reach.

Nice extras

A free TMDB key turns on Smart filters, an MDBList key adds ratings, and a Gemini key turns on the copilot.

FAQ

Good questions

Does HomeBot Cinema host or provide any media?

No. It does not host, index, provide or recommend any media. It is a client for the sources you configure and a manager for your own server. You decide what to point it at, and you are responsible for following the laws and terms that apply to you. Use it for media you have the right to access.

Do I need a Stremio account?

No. Sign-in goes through Cloudflare Access, and the app never calls Stremio's account API. It does use Stremio's public Cinemeta catalogs and artwork; every outside service is listed in docs/third-party-services.md.

What hardware do I need?

Anything that runs Docker: a Mac mini, a NAS or a mini PC, plus disk space for your library. The macOS app needs Apple Silicon.

Is it safe to reach from outside my home?

Behind Cloudflare Access, only the people you invite can sign in, and the server refuses every request without a valid token. Admin rights belong to the sign-ins you list, not to a profile.

Can I turn the AI off?

It is off by default. The copilot runs only when you enable the ai plugin and add a Gemini key, and even then it can only stage actions that a person confirms.

Is it affiliated with Stremio?

No. HomeBot Cinema is an independent fork of Stremio Web (GPL-2.0) and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Stremio or Smart Code OOD.
